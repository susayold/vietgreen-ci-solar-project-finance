# DATA_DICTIONARY

`project_master`: synthetic opportunity identity, geography, capacity, risk, gates and economics.
`offtaker_master`: simulated customer proxies and internal credit grade; not external ratings.
`site_risk`: roof-right, structural, continuity, fire and insurance fields.
`solar_resource`: PVOUT screening anchor and source chain.
`ppa_terms`, `capex`, `opex`, `debt_terms`: simulated commercial/finance inputs with IDs.

No real customer identifiers, personal data, credentials, proprietary quotes or hidden truth are stored publicly.
