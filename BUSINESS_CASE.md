# Business Case — V5.1.3

VietGreen’s use case is a source-backed diligence and recruiter-facing comparison of publicly disclosed C&I/distributed-solar projects. The value is a transparent common data model and repeatable economics layer, not a claim that confidential transaction data is available.

Observed capacity, generation and source lineage remain in the public master; modeled load, self-consumption, CAPEX and underwriting terms remain explicit overlay inputs. A resolved model-input view records the final field, origin and readiness decision. Physical QA fails closed for missing data and blocks the preserved Arisudhana outlier from direct base economics.

The output is a diligence-priority and commercial-negotiation shortlist. PPA is `FRONTIER_ONLY`, exact PPA is not disclosed, capital allocation is disabled, and the decision boundary remains `INDETERMINATE_MISSING_COMMERCIAL_DATA`. No output is an investment portfolio, lender commitment, bankability conclusion or approval.
