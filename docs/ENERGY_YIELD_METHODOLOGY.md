# ENERGY_YIELD_METHODOLOGY

PVOUT is used as modeled output; no second generic PR. P50 = capacity × PVOUT. P90 = P50 × (1 − 1.2816 × uncertainty). This is an analytical approximation, not a bankable yield assessment.

Expected losses and uncertainty are separate; degradation is applied after year 1; P90 ≤ P50 is a blocker test.
