# FX_FINANCING_METHODOLOGY

The base model is VND. A USD-debt fraction is translated period-by-period at a base VND/USD rate.

For depreciation rate d:

FX_t = FX_0 × (1 + d)^t

The remote output reports translated debt service under zero, 2%, 4% and 6% annual VND depreciation paths, plus one-off shock configurations in the scenario register. FX stress is a diagnostic, not a forecast or hedge valuation.

Before any foreign borrowing claim, the checklist requires confirmation of permitted borrower/use, registration/reporting, lender/security structure, account mechanics, tax/withholding and enforceability.
