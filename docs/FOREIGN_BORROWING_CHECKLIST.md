# FOREIGN_BORROWING_CHECKLIST

Foreign borrowing is a legal/documentary gate, not an assumed funding source. Before using USD debt in a real transaction, confirm:

- eligible borrower and permitted purpose;
- registration, reporting and account requirements;
- lender, security, guarantee and enforcement structure;
- payment, withholding-tax and hedging mechanics;
- FX convertibility and reserve policy;
- covenant, DSRA and cash-waterfall compatibility;
- governing law, dispute forum and enforceability;
- evidence owner, reviewer, effective date and recheck date.

The current FX output is translation stress only. It does not establish legal eligibility or hedge economics.
