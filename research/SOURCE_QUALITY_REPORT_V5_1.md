## Evidence semantics and missing data

Source quality and score-derived candidate coverage are separate. Observed facts, derived facts, benchmark reconstructions and analyst assumptions are separate. Exact PPA, lender terms, site rights, tax opinion and bankable technical P90 are not public in the selected source set. V5.1 therefore uses FRONTIER_ONLY PPA logic and standardized underwriting, never actual lender claims.

## Benchmark dependency and granularity

CAPEX/OPEX rows retain source value, source currency, source unit, source basis, FX, normalized value/currency/unit, benchmark ID and comparability grade. Portfolio facts remain portfolio facts; no platform/program total is converted to a fictitious site asset.
