# Screening / Diligence Committee Memo — V5.1.1

This is standardized public-data screening, not investment committee approval. Recommendation classes are ADVANCE_TO_COMMERCIAL_DILIGENCE, ADVANCE_WITH_CONDITIONS, HOLD and DROP_FROM_SHORTLIST; INVEST is not used while exact PPA data is missing.

The 20-project output is a DILIGENCE_PRIORITY_SHORTLIST / COMMERCIAL_NEGOTIATION_SHORTLIST. Review evidence grade, observed capacity/generation, customer ceiling, leveraged Sponsor Floor, Lender Floor, zone status, standardized debt capacity and missing commercial evidence before any actual decision.

Decision boundary: INDETERMINATE_MISSING_COMMERCIAL_DATA.


Scenario results should be read as screening stress against contractual debt service, not as an assumed refinancing or lender waiver.
