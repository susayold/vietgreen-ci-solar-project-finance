The released V4 candidate screens 20 synthetic projects. Current Terms produce 0 / 20 positive Equity NPV rows, so the current decision is NO_DEPLOYMENT. The negotiated hypothetical base case has Project NPV of 5.262393 BVND, Equity NPV of 5.942277 BVND, Project IRR of 12.732% and Equity IRR of 15.929%.

The downside boundary is explicit. P90 Equity NPV is -1.177896 BVND, CAPEX-overrun Equity NPV is -3.179160 BVND and combined-downside Equity NPV is -38.814456 BVND. Credit covenant status and sponsor economics are reported separately; a DSCR PASS is not an Equity NPV PASS.

This is a recruiter-ready synthetic package, not a transaction approval. It is not a bankable P90 case, lender-approved model, legal/tax opinion, technical certification, site diligence package or IC approval. Negotiated terms are hypothetical and do not represent executed contracts. Eight external evidence gates remain open.
