# Executive summary

VietGreen CI Solar Project Finance is a synthetic, remote-reproducible candidate for screening a Vietnam C&I solar portfolio.

The current run evaluates 20 projects, passes 12 eligibility gates, and selects 11 projects totaling 13.4 MWp. The endogenous pooled facility sizes at 152.880435 BVND against 131.869565 BVND of equity, with a pooled DSCR of 1.30x. The base sponsor NPV is negative at -59.736498 BVND, so the economic case is conditional and requires pricing, capex, structure or risk improvement before approval.

The engine now executes an 8,760-hour profile for every project, carries P50/P90 energy through annual cash flow, includes tax/VAT/working capital/maintenance and zero terminal value, sizes debt using multiple coverage concepts, resizes pooled debt with feedback, and tests FX, default, site, common-factor and isolation cases.

The candidate is suitable for review, challenge and further diligence. It is not a final investment recommendation, lender-approved model, legal/tax opinion, bankable P90 case or recruiter-ready portfolio. Tariff time windows are legally effective metadata, but billed implementation remains WATCH pending confirmation through the official regulatory and implementation chain.
